📱 *Trackly - Your Expense Lens*
Track every expense in seconds.

👉 *Try it:* https://pietrozambo-tech.github.io/expense-tracker/

*Install it (30 seconds)*
Open the link on your iPhone in Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*. It then behaves like a real app and works offline.

*Start here*
Pick your name and main currency (~150 available), then tap *Load sample data* on the welcome tour - it fills the app with realistic transactions so you can play with every chart straight away. Remove it in one tap when you're done; it never touches your own data.
Sign in with Google to sync across devices, or just use it as a guest - your call.

*What to try*

📊 *Dashboard* - spending, income, savings and saving rate for any month, quarter or year. Set a monthly budget in Profile and a bar tells you if you're on track or burning it too fast. Tap a category → see its subcategories → tap one → the actual transactions behind the number. Plus charts for cumulative spending, recurring vs one-off, and spending by bank/card.

🧾 *Activity* - every transaction grouped by day. Filter by month, category, source or recurring, or just search. Tap to edit, swipe left to delete, export to CSV.

➕ *Add* - amount first, then category (with subcategories), date and which card it came from. A few seconds per expense.

🔁 *Recurring* - set the rent once and it appears automatically on the day it's due. Change it later and the app asks: only this one, or this and all future ones?

📈 *Trend* - the long view: monthly totals, average, best and worst month, and the trend of any single category. Plus a savings view with your saving rate.

⚙️ *Settings* - build your own categories (icons, colours, subcategories), add your banks and cards, switch main currency, export a full backup.

💱 *Travelling?* Log an expense in yen or pesos - your dashboard stays in your own currency. Each transaction locks its exchange rate on its own date, so old numbers never drift.

📥 *Got history in a spreadsheet?* Settings → Import data gives you a ready-made AI prompt: paste it into ChatGPT/Claude with your file - even a bank statement or screenshots - and import the result.

🐞 *Found something odd?*
Settings → *Support* sends me a message straight from the app. Bugs, confusing bits, missing features - all welcome.

Thanks for testing 🙏
